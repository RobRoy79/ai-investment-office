# AI Investment Office

An AI-powered investment system designed to
analyze stock markets and identify investment
opportunities.

## Initial Investment
10,000 SEK

## Market
Stocks

## Trading Strategy
Automated trading with AI-driven analysis.

## AI Agents

1. Market Analyst
   Analyzes stock market trends.

2. Stock Research Agent
   Finds promising stocks.

3. Risk Manager
   Evaluates risks and protects capital.

4. Trading Agent
   Generates buy and sell signals.

5. Portfolio Manager
   Monitors portfolio performance.

## Risk Management

- Protect initial capital.
- Limit trading risks.
- Monitor all positions.
- Start with paper trading.
- Require approval before real trades.

## Mission

Build an intelligent, low-cost AI investment
office capable of analyzing markets and
managing investments efficiently.
